# Evaluation questionnaire, 22 October 2026

The paper contained an evaluation plan and sample indicators, but no complete prepared questionnaire. At the user's request, added the EVALUATION QUESTIONNAIRE tab (t.ekvdofjvvaa4) to the existing Google Doc, with a direct link from Chapter 3, Evaluation Procedure > Instrument and Evidence.

## Instrument

- Appendix A: 25 original BuildTrack statements, five each for functional suitability, performance efficiency, interaction capability, reliability and security.
- Session fields, role-specific task directions, a five-point agreement scale, N/O handling and open comments.
- Appendix B: eight technical verification checks covering selected information-security review areas and supporting performance/recovery evidence. Technical outcomes remain separate from questionnaire ratings.
- Researcher administration/scoring guide: valid-response frequencies, percentages, means and sample standard deviations; proposed minimum three answered items for a participant-level characteristic mean, subject to adviser approval before collection. No inferential test or respondent count invented.

The supplied IAS2_Module-ISO-25010.pdf identifies ISO/IEC 25010:2023 and explains that the quality model is not a ready-made questionnaire. Its MatatagVPN examples were not copied as BuildTrack functions. Chapter 1 objectives and Chapter 3 already select the same five characteristics; two remaining Chapter 1 references to usability were changed to interaction capability for consistency with the 2023 edition. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is used only for the selected project-level review, not an ISMS certification claim. Official standard metadata was checked against ISO and IEC pages linked in the instrument.

The instrument remains a researcher-developed draft for adviser review and trial administration. It is not represented as validated. Physical biometric enrollment/attendance remain unassessed until real connection and supervised testing. Perceived responsiveness is separated from timing/resource/capacity measurements. No ratings or technical results were fabricated.
